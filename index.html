About the Destination

Nature. Culture. Adventure.

Paradise Cove is a tropical destination blessed with natural beauty, rich history, and vibrant culture. It is a place where mountains meet the sea, creating unforgettable experiences for every traveler.

Natural Beauty Local Culture Adventure

Paradise Cove offers a relaxing escape while allowing visitors to experience nature, explore local traditions, enjoy delicious food, and discover exciting outdoor activities.

Visitors can enjoy beaches, scenic landscapes, cultural experiences, local cuisine, and outdoor adventures surrounded by beautiful tropical scenery.

Top Attractions

Explore the Best of Paradise Cove

Discover breathtaking beaches, lagoons, waterfalls, marine sanctuaries, and viewpoints inspired by some of the country's most iconic destinations.

Crystal Beach

Inspired by Crystal Beach Resort, San Narciso, Zambales. A beautiful white sand beach known for surfing, relaxing sunsets, and crystal clear waters.

Secret Lagoon

Inspired by Secret Lagoon, El Nido, Palawan. A hidden paradise surrounded by towering limestone cliffs and turquoise waters.

Tuklas Falls

Inspired by Kawasan Falls, Badian, Cebu. Famous for its turquoise waters, scenic cascades, and thrilling canyoneering experience.

Sunset Cove

Inspired by Nacpan Beach, El Nido, Palawan. Famous for its golden shoreline and breathtaking sunset views over the sea.

Marine Sanctuary

Inspired by Apo Island Marine Reserve, Dauin, Negros Oriental. Known for its vibrant coral gardens, sea turtles, and exceptional marine biodiversity.

Island Viewpoint

Inspired by Taraw Cliff, El Nido, Palawan. Famous for its stunning panoramic views of El Nido's limestone cliffs, islands, and crystal clear waters.

Information Alert: Best time to visit is November to May

Discover The Philippine Paradise

Explore beautiful destinations, breathtaking views, and unforgettable moments from Philippine Paradise.

El Nido

Famous for its towering limestone karst formations and hidden emerald lagoons. Must-visit spots include the Big Lagoon and Small Lagoon, which are best explored by kayak.

Coron Palawan

Positioned just north of mainland Palawan, it is a paradise for snorkelers and divers. It features the breathtakingly clear freshwater Kayangan Lake and pristine shipwrecks from WWII.

Bohol

A diverse island where you can witness the iconic Chocolate Hills—over 1,000 symmetrical mounds that turn brown during the dry season—and visit protected sanctuaries for the tiny, big-eyed tarsier

Batanes

Situated at the northernmost tip of the country, Batanes features dramatic rolling green hills, rock-hewn cliffs, traditional stone houses, and a slow, peaceful lifestyle.

Siquijor

A quiet island province surrounded by white beaches and mysterious local folklore. Highlights include swinging into the turquoise waters of Cambugahay Falls.

Camiguin

Known as the "Island Born of Fire," this volcanic destination features an amazing sunken cemetery, natural hot and cold springs, and the completely bare White Island sandbar.
